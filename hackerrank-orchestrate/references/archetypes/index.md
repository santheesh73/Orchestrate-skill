# Problem archetypes

Each edition's problem has had one dominant shape. Once the problem statement drops, pick the closest page, say which and why in the transcript, and say so plainly when none fits. A new problem may combine shapes (for example retrieval plus multimodal evidence); take the architecture from the dominant one and the guards from each.

| Page | Shape | Seen in | Tell-tale signs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `retrieval-triage.md` | Answer or route requests from a document corpus | May 2026 (support triage) | A corpus of articles, tickets to reply to or escalate, cite-your-source columns |
| `multimodal-verify.md` | Judge a claim against images or documents | June 2026 | Images per row, verdict classes including "not enough information" |
| `adversarial-routing.md` | Route or mute messages, some hostile | August 2026 | Mixed media with wrong extensions, impersonation, text addressing the system |
| `simulation-planning.md` | Compute a safe plan from structured records over time | September 2026 (Buy or Wait?) | Profiles, dated events, forecast windows, plan columns, tie-break orders |

Each page gives: the architecture that held up, the guards that fit, the grounding test, and where rows are usually lost.
